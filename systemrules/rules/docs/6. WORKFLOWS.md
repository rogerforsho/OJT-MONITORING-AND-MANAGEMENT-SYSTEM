# System Workflows

## 1. Student Registration & Approval
1. Student (4th-year ICS or IBE) submits registration details (Full Name, Email, Password, Student Number, Course, Year Level) via the web portal or mobile app.
2. System securely creates the authentication record and marks account status as `pending`.
3. Student profile is created with student identity information.
4. OJT Coordinator inspects pending registrations on the web dashboard.
5. Coordinator verifies student legitimacy and either **Approves** (status becomes `active`) or **Rejects** (status becomes `rejected`).
6. Approved students sign in exclusively via the mobile app. (Web login is restricted to institutional staff, supervisors, and administrators; students attempting web login are instructed to open the mobile app).

## 2. Pre-Deployment Requirements & OJT Application
1. Student accesses pre-deployment requirements section on the mobile app.
2. Student uploads mandatory gateway requirements:
   - **Certificate of Completion of Pre-OJT Orientation** (validates attendance at institutional orientation seminar).
   - **Validated Student ID Card** (front and back scan verifying active 4th-year enrollment in ICS / IBE).
   - Institutional Placement Form, Medical Clearance, and Notarized Student Waiver.
3. Coordinator reviews and verifies submitted requirement documents on the web dashboard.
4. Once all gateway requirements are approved, the student unlocks eligibility for schedule configuration and company assignment.

## 3. Practicum Schedule Commitment & Company Assignment
1. Student fills out the proposed **Practicum Work Schedule** (practicum start and end dates, weekly work days, daily shift time in/out, lunch break, and work modality).
2. Coordinator reviews the submitted schedule against institutional/CHED guidelines (max 8 hours/day, max 40 hours/week, no graveyard shifts) and company working hours.
3. Coordinator approves or adjusts the schedule, links the partner company record, and designates the Company Supervisor.
4. Approved schedule locks in the attendance engine parameters (late cutoff threshold, daily rendered hour cap, and weekend permission rules).

## 4. Attendance Logging, Selfie Evidence & GPS Geotagging
1. Student signs in on the mobile app.
2. Student taps **Time In**, triggering a point-in-time device GPS fix (latitude, longitude, accuracy radius, and satellite timestamp).
3. Mobile camera activates live front-facing capture with an oval viewfinder guide and **on-device face presence validation** (prevents covered lens, blank walls, or random objects; gallery/photo picker upload is disallowed). An anti-spoofing watermark HUD (timestamp, GPS coordinates, student name, and student ID) is embedded.
4. If the student is on an official errand, field assignment, or remote task outside company premises, the student includes an activity note for supervisor context.
5. Backend validates active assignment, duplicate check (one session per day), weekend rules, and determines late status based on the verified schedule cutoff.
6. Record is created with `verification_status: 'pending'`, selfie saved to private Supabase storage, and GPS coordinates stored with the session metadata.
7. At the end of the shift, student taps **Time Out**, captures a Time Out selfie with on-device face presence validation and point-in-time GPS geotagging, and submits.
8. Company Supervisor reviews pending attendance on the supervisor dashboard using **Smart Compliance Triage**:
   - Automated compliance badges display status: 🟢 Face Presence Confirmed, 🟢 GPS In-Bounds, 🟢 On-Time Shift.
   - Supervisor can perform **1-Click Batch Verification** for all clean logs, reserving manual inspection only for flagged exceptions or errand notes.
9. Verified attendance automatically calculates rendered hours and updates internship progress in real-time.

## 5. Digital Report Submission & Review
1. Student prepares periodic OJT report (e.g., weekly journal, accomplishment report).
2. Student uploads the report file (PDF/Image) with remarks.
3. Coordinator reviews the submission on the coordinator dashboard.
4. Coordinator marks the report as **Reviewed**, **Approved**, or **Rejected** with feedback remarks.
5. Student receives immediate status feedback and remarks.

## 6. Performance Evaluation
1. Company Supervisor opens the assigned trainee's evaluation module.
2. Supervisor completes the institutional evaluation form with rating scores and qualitative feedback.
3. System validates required rubrics and stores the evaluation.
4. Coordinator accesses submitted evaluations for academic grading.

## 7. Notifications & Announcements
1. System generates automated event notifications (e.g., account approved, attendance verified, report reviewed).
2. System Administrator or Coordinator drafts and broadcasts announcements to target user roles.
3. Users receive notifications and view announcements on their dashboards.

## 8. Internship Completion & Record Finalization
1. System tracks accumulated rendered hours against required program hours (e.g., ICS vs IBE hours).
2. When required hours and all mandatory reports are approved, the Coordinator finalizes the internship.
3. All student attendance, reports, and evaluations become permanently **read-only** historical records.
