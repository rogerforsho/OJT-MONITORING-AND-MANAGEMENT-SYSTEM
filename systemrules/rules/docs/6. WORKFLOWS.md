# System Workflows

## 1. Student Registration & Approval
1. Student (4th-year ICS or IBE) submits registration details (Full Name, Email, Password, Student Number, Course, Year Level).
2. System securely creates the authentication record and marks account status as `pending`.
3. Student profile is created with student identity information.
4. OJT Coordinator inspects pending registrations on the web dashboard.
5. Coordinator verifies student legitimacy and either **Approves** (status becomes `active`) or **Rejects** (status becomes `rejected`).
6. Approved students can sign in to web and mobile apps.

## 2. Pre-Deployment Requirements & OJT Application
1. Student accesses pre-deployment requirements section on mobile or web.
2. Student uploads digital copies of required institutional forms (e.g., Placement Form, Waiver, Student ID).
3. Coordinator verifies submitted requirement documents.
4. Once verified, student is marked eligible for company assignment.

## 3. Company & Supervisor Assignment
1. Coordinator creates/updates partner company record and defines approved working schedule.
2. Coordinator creates supervisor account for the partner company.
3. Coordinator assigns eligible student to the company and designates the company supervisor.
4. Assignment unlocks the student's attendance capability and the supervisor's trainee roster.

## 4. Online Attendance & Selfie Verification
1. Student signs in on the mobile app (requires active internet connection).
2. Student taps **Time In**, captures a live front-camera selfie as visual evidence, and submits.
3. Backend validates active assignment, duplicate check (one session per day), weekend rules, and determines late status based on company work schedule cutoff.
4. Record is created with `verification_status: 'pending'` and selfie saved to private Supabase storage.
5. At the end of the shift, student taps **Time Out**, captures a Time Out selfie, and submits.
6. Company Supervisor reviews the pending attendance record and visual selfie evidence on the supervisor dashboard.
7. Supervisor marks the record as **Verified** or **Rejected**.
8. Verified attendance automatically calculates rendered hours and updates internship progress.

## 5. Digital Report Submission & Review
1. Student prepares periodic OJT report (e.g., weekly journal, accomplishment report).
2. Student uploads the report file (PDF/Image) with remarks.
3. Coordinator reviews the submission on the coordinator dashboard.
4. Coordinator marks the report as **Reviewed**, **Approved**, or **Rejected** with feedback remarks.
5. Student receives immediate status feedback and remarks.

## 6. Performance Evaluation
1. Company Supervisor opens the assigned trainee's evaluation module.
2. Supervisor completes the institutional evaluation form with rating scores and qualitative feedback.
3. System validates required rubrics and stores the evaluation.
4. Coordinator accesses submitted evaluations for academic grading.

## 7. Notifications & Announcements
1. System generates automated event notifications (e.g., account approved, attendance verified, report reviewed).
2. System Administrator or Coordinator drafts and broadcasts announcements to target user roles.
3. Users receive notifications and view announcements on their dashboards.

## 8. Internship Completion & Record Finalization
1. System tracks accumulated rendered hours against required program hours (e.g., ICS vs IBE hours).
2. When required hours and all mandatory reports are approved, the Coordinator finalizes the internship.
3. All student attendance, reports, and evaluations become permanently **read-only** historical records.
