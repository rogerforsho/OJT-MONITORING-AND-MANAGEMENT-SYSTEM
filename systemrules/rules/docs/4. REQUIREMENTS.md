# Requirements Specification

Labels: **RV** research-validated; **UA** user-approved; **TD** technical design decision.

## Authentication & Role Management
- **FR-AUTH-001 [UA]** Fourth-year students from ICS and IBE may self-register.
- **FR-AUTH-002 [UA]** New student accounts require authorized approval before gaining access.
- **FR-AUTH-003 [UA]** The OJT Coordinator approves legitimate student registrations.
- **FR-AUTH-004 [UA]** Student identity data includes email, student number, course, and year level.
- **FR-AUTH-005 [UA]** Each user is assigned exactly one role (Student, Coordinator, Supervisor, ProgramHead, Admin).
- **FR-AUTH-006 [UA]** System Administrator manages technical account activation/deactivation and access administration.
- **FR-AUTH-007 [TD]** Password recovery uses secure email-based reset through Supabase Auth.

## Student Profile & OJT Application
- **FR-APP-001 [UA]** Students must complete student profile details before deployment.
- **FR-APP-002 [UA]** Students submit pre-deployment requirements digitally (Placement Form, Waiver, Student ID, etc.).
- **FR-APP-003 [UA]** Coordinator reviews and verifies pre-deployment requirements before assigning students.

## Companies & Assignments
- **FR-COMP-001 [UA]** Coordinator manages company records and partner profiles.
- **FR-COMP-002 [UA]** Coordinator assigns eligible students to partner companies.
- **FR-COMP-003 [UA]** Coordinator assigns company supervisors to students.
- **FR-COMP-004 [UA]** A company may have multiple trainees.
- **FR-COMP-005 [UA]** A supervisor may supervise multiple assigned trainees.
- **FR-COMP-006 [UA]** Company reassignments or modifications are managed exclusively by the Coordinator.

## Attendance & Selfie Verification
- **FR-ATT-001 [RV/UA]** Support online attendance monitoring for web and mobile.
- **FR-ATT-002 [TD]** Normal attendance cycle: Time In → OJT Shift → Time Out.
- **FR-ATT-003 [TD]** One attendance session per approved OJT day.
- **FR-ATT-004 [UA]** Students cannot directly edit verified or finalized attendance records.
- **FR-ATT-005 [UA]** Weekend attendance is rejected by default unless institutionally approved.
- **FR-ATT-006 [UA]** System determines late status based on company work schedule cutoff time.
- **FR-ATT-007 [TD]** Late status requires an assigned schedule; a total-hour goal alone cannot determine lateness.
- **FR-ATT-008 [UA]** Attendance recording requires an active internet connection (the system does not support offline attendance per manuscript specifications).
- **FR-SELFIE-001 [UA]** Selfie capture is manual visual evidence for supervisor review (no facial recognition).
- **FR-SELFIE-002 [TD]** Selfie is required during Time In and Time Out.
- **FR-SELFIE-003 [UA]** Assigned Company Supervisor inspects and manually verifies selfie evidence.
- **FR-SELFIE-004 [UA]** Unverified or rejected attendance is not credited toward rendered hours.

## Progress & Hour Computation
- **FR-PROG-001 [RV]** Automatically compute rendered and remaining internship hours in real-time.
- **FR-PROG-002 [RV]** Display rendered hours, remaining hours, and progress percentage to students and coordinators.
- **FR-PROG-003 [TD]** Credited hours derive exclusively from verified, approved attendance records.
- **FR-PROG-004 [TD]** Required hours are configurable per program/assignment (e.g., ICS vs IBE hours).
- **FR-PROG-005 [UA]** Finalized internship records become read-only upon completion.

## Digital Reports & Requirements Submission
- **FR-REP-001 [RV]** Students submit weekly/monthly OJT accomplishment reports digitally.
- **FR-REP-002 [RV]** Real-time submission status visibility (submitted, reviewed, approved, rejected) with remarks.
- **FR-REP-003 [TD]** Uploaded files are validated for size and approved file formats (e.g., PDF, PNG, JPG).
- **FR-REP-004 [UA]** Coordinator reviews, remarks, and approves submitted reports.

## Performance Evaluation
- **FR-EVAL-001 [UA]** Company Supervisors evaluate assigned students' performance.
- **FR-EVAL-002 [TD]** Evaluation criteria adhere to the approved institutional evaluation form and scoring rubrics.
- **FR-EVAL-003 [UA]** Coordinators monitor and compile submitted evaluations for grading.

## Notifications & Announcements
- **FR-NOTIF-001 [RV]** Centralized notification system for status updates, approval alerts, and review feedback.
- **FR-ANNC-001 [UA]** System Administrator and Coordinators can create and broadcast institutional announcements.

## Monitoring & Administrative Reports
- **FR-MON-001 [UA]** Program Head accesses department-level monitoring summaries (ICS / IBE).
- **FR-MON-002 [TD]** All metrics and summaries are computed from live operational data.
- **FR-ADMIN-001 [UA]** System Administrator manages user accounts, system configuration, and announcements.
- **FR-ADMIN-002 [TD]** Administrator privileges remain distinct from academic OJT approval authority.

## Non-Functional & Evaluation Standards (ISO/IEC 25010:2023)
- **Functional Suitability:** All 12 core functional areas operate as specified in the capstone manuscript.
- **Performance Efficiency:** Fast load times, indexed queries, and minimal bandwidth consumption on 8 GB RAM dev environments.
- **Usability:** Clean, intuitive UI/UX with responsive web access and native mobile controls.
- **Security:** Strict Row-Level Security (RLS), signed storage URLs, secure password handling. *(Reported as "security-related features were evaluated".)*
