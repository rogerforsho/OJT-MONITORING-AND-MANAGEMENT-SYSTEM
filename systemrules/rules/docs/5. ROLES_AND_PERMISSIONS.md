## Role Boundaries
- **Student:** Restricted to 4th-year ICS & IBE students at Colegio de Montalban. Access is confined strictly to personal records.
- **Coordinator:** Academic management authority for approvals, assignments, requirements, attendance validation, and progress tracking.
- **Supervisor:** Access is strictly scoped to assigned trainees at the partner company. Chat is limited to active Coordinators whose department matches the course department of at least one of the Supervisor’s active trainees.
- **Coordinator chat scope:** An active Coordinator may chat with Supervisors who have at least one active trainee whose course department matches the Coordinator’s department. Admins and Program Heads cannot read or send chat messages.
- **Program Head:** Strategic, department-level oversight and summary monitoring without altering individual transactional records.
- **Admin:** Technical administration (account status, announcements, configuration). Does not have academic decision authority.
- **IT Experts / Evaluators:** External technical reviewers who evaluate the platform according to ISO/IEC 25010:2023 quality standards.
