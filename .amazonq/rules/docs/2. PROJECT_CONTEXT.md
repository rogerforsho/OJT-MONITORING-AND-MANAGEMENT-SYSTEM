# Project Context

## Project
**Cross-Platform On-the-Job Training (OJT) Monitoring and Management System**

## Research Context
Supplied feedback from OJT students, primarily BSIT and BSBA third- and fourth-year students at Colegio de Montalban, describes a fragmented process involving:
- printed documents;
- manual physical submission;
- paper logbooks and DTR;
- spreadsheets;
- Google Forms;
- Messenger and similar communication channels.

## Research-Validated Problems
- lost physical documents;
- delayed approvals;
- slow or fragmented communication;
- difficult submission processes;
- reliance on manual attendance records;
- difficulty monitoring internship progress.

## Research-Validated Needs
- online attendance monitoring;
- centralized digital submissions;
- centralized records;
- internship-hour progress tracking;
- notifications or centralized updates;
- better visibility of submission and approval status.

## Evidence Boundary
The supplied student feedback supports **online attendance**, but does not by itself prove that students specifically requested:
- selfie evidence;
- a native desktop application;
- unspecified analytics.

selfie evidence are approved system design decisions, not direct survey findings unless separate evidence is later supplied.

## Approved Roles
- Student
- OJT Coordinator
- Company Supervisor
- Program Head
- Admin

The Admin is a technical/system administration role and does not replace academic OJT management roles.
