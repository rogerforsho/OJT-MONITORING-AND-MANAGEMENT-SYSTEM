# Product Requirements Document
## Version 1.0

## Product Overview

The Cross-Platform On-the-Job Training (OJT) Monitoring and Management System is a centralized platform designed to support the management, monitoring, and documentation of internship activities for Colegio de Montalban.

The system replaces fragmented manual processes involving physical documents, paper attendance records, spreadsheets, online forms, and messaging applications with a single centralized platform accessible through a mobile application and a responsive web application connected to one backend.

## Goals
- centralize OJT records;
- support online attendance;
- selfie evidence;
- track rendered and remaining internship hours;
- centralize report/requirement submission;
- support evaluation management;
- provide status visibility and notifications;
- provide role-appropriate monitoring;
- operate through mobile and responsive web clients using one centralized backend.

## Non-Goals
- separate native desktop application;
- automated facial recognition;
- unapproved private chat;
- invented analytics;
- unrelated school-management functions;
- duplicate backend platforms.

## Users

### Student
Self-registers, waits for approval, records attendance, views progress, submits approved requirements, and receives notifications.

### OJT Coordinator
Account is created by the Admin.
Approves legitimate student registrations, manages companies and assignments, monitors students, and performs approved OJT management workflows.

### Company Supervisor
Account is created by the OJT Coordinator after company assignment.
Accesses assigned students, manually reviews attendance selfie evidence, participates in attendance verification, and submits evaluations.

### Program Head
Account is created by the OJT Coordinator after company assignment.
Views approved high-level monitoring information and summaries.

### Admin
Manages privileged user accounts (Coordinators, Program Heads, and other Admins if permitted) and provides technical system administration.
Performs technical account/access administration and system support without automatically receiving academic decision authority.

Pre-Deployment Requirements

Before a student becomes eligible for OJT deployment, required institutional documents shall be completed according to school policy.

Examples include:

- Placement Form
- Waiver
- Student ID
- Other institutional requirements
Attendance validation shall support institution-approved company work schedules where applicable.
## Functional Requirements

The system shall:

- Authenticate users.
- Require coordinator approval before student activation.
- Support role-based access control.
- Allow students to record attendance.
- Support selfie evidence submission.
- Synchronize offline attendance when connectivity is restored.
- Track internship hours.
- Track internship progress.
- Allow report and requirement submission.
- Support evaluation management.
- Notify users of important events.
- Maintain historical internship records.
### Completed Internship Records

Once a student's internship is officially completed and finalized by the authorized personnel, internship records shall become read-only.

Historical internship records shall remain accessible to authorized users for viewing and reporting purposes but shall no longer be editable through normal system operations.

Any modification after finalization shall require an authorized administrative process in accordance with institutional policy.

### Attendance Validation

The system shall validate attendance based on the approved work schedule assigned to each student's internship company where applicable.

Attendance validation shall consider the company's approved working hours during verification to ensure accurate recording of internship attendance.
### Attendance Record Finalization
Attendance records that have been verified and finalized shall become read-only.

Corrections to finalized attendance records shall only be performed by authorized personnel following the institution's approved procedures.

## Success Criteria

Version 1.0 is considered successful when:

- Approved OJT records are managed through one centralized system.
- Students can record attendance using the approved attendance workflow.
- OJT Coordinators can monitor internship progress from the web application.
- Company Supervisors can submit evaluations for assigned students.
- Students can submit approved reports and requirements digitally.
- Authorized users can monitor internship hours and completion status.
- Role-based access control is enforced for all system users.
