# Requirements

Labels: **RV** research-validated; **UA** user-approved; **TD** technical design decision.

## Authentication
- **FR-AUTH-001 [UA]** Students may self-register.
- **FR-AUTH-002 [UA]** New student accounts require authorized approval.
- **FR-AUTH-003 [UA]** The OJT Coordinator approves legitimate student registrations.
- **FR-AUTH-004 [UA]** Student identity data includes email and student number.
- **FR-AUTH-005 [UA]** One user has one role.
- **FR-AUTH-006 [UA]** Admin manages technical account activation/deactivation and access administration.
- **FR-AUTH-007 [TD]** Password recovery uses secure email-based reset through the approved authentication provider.

## Companies and Assignments
- **FR-COMP-001 [UA]** Coordinator manages company records.
- **FR-COMP-002 [UA]** Coordinator assigns students to companies.
- **FR-COMP-003 [UA]** Coordinator assigns supervisors to students.
- **FR-COMP-004 [UA]** A company may have many students.
- **FR-COMP-005 [UA]** A supervisor may supervise many students.
- **FR-COMP-006 [UA]** Company changes are managed/approved by the Coordinator.

## Attendance
- **FR-ATT-001 [RV/UA]** Support online attendance monitoring.
- **FR-ATT-002 [TD]** Normal cycle: Time In → OJT work → Time Out.
- **FR-ATT-003 [TD]** One primary attendance session per approved OJT day unless later policy requires split shifts.
- **FR-ATT-004 [UA]** Students cannot directly edit finalized attendance.
- **FR-ATT-005 [UA]** Weekend attendance is not allowed in Version 1.0.
- **FR-ATT-006 [UA]** Support late-status determination.
- **FR-ATT-007 [TD]** Late status requires an assigned schedule; a total-hour goal alone cannot determine lateness.
- **FR-ATT-008 [UA/TD]** Attendance capture may be stored locally while offline and synchronized when connectivity returns.
- **FR-ATT-009 [TD]** Offline events retain original capture time and synchronization state.
- **FR-ATT-010 [TD]** Synchronization must not silently overwrite conflicting server records.



## Selfie Evidence
- **FR-SELFIE-001 [UA]** Selfie is manual evidence; no facial recognition.
- **FR-SELFIE-002 [TD]** Selfie is required at Time In and Time Out.
- **FR-SELFIE-003 [UA]** Assigned Company Supervisor may view the evidence.
- **FR-SELFIE-004 [UA]** Company Supervisor manually verifies it.
- **FR-SELFIE-005 [UA]** Failed required verification means attendance is not credited as valid.
- **FR-SELFIE-006 [TD]** Retention follows approved institutional policy; no legal retention period may be invented.

## Progress
- **FR-PROG-001 [RV]** Track internship progress.
- **FR-PROG-002 [RV]** Show rendered and remaining hours.
- **FR-PROG-003 [TD]** Credited hours derive from valid attendance.
- **FR-PROG-004 [TD]** Required hours are configurable by applicable program/assignment, not guessed or hard-coded.

## Reports
- **FR-REP-001 [RV]** Students submit approved OJT reports/requirements digitally.
- **FR-REP-002 [RV]** Show submission status.
- **FR-REP-003 [TD]** Exact types, limits, deadlines, statuses, and review rules require institutional definition.

## Evaluation
- **FR-EVAL-001 [UA]** Supervisors evaluate assigned students.
- **FR-EVAL-002 [TD]** Criteria must follow the approved institutional evaluation form.

## Notifications
- **FR-NOTIF-001 [RV]** Provide centralized notifications/announcements for approved events.
- **FR-NOTIF-002 [TD]** Private chat is excluded unless separately approved.

## Monitoring and Admin
- **FR-MON-001 [UA]** Program Head receives role-appropriate monitoring access.
- **FR-MON-002 [TD]** Metrics must come from real system data and approved definitions.
- **FR-ADMIN-001 [UA]** Include Admin for technical account/access administration.
- **FR-ADMIN-002 [TD]** Admin authority remains separate from academic OJT approval unless explicitly granted.

## Non-Functional
- server-side authorization;
- centralized persistent source of truth;
- responsive desktop/laptop web access;
- mobile camera support for approved workflows;
- protected sensitive data;
- clear loading, empty, error, offline, and sync states;
- practical development on an 8 GB RAM machine.
